Watch first. Then take it apart.

The final 59.75-second cut, in Korean with English subtitles. Each chapter button plays one part and stops at its end.

02 / THE BRIEF

What we borrowed. What we made ours.

Each viral reel was studied for its mechanism: camera, framing, timing and the moment people share. That mechanism was rebuilt with our own family and story. No frame of anyone else's video is in the film or the project.

REFERENCESFive viral formatsA door into a painted world, multiplicity, flying into a painting, the giant behind you, one final hug.
OUR IDEAA boy's drawings come aliveStarring Danney's sons Jason and Kayden, their dad and Boba the cat.
OUR DIRECTIONCute → apocalypseWonder first, then the city breaks, then a sad giant and one last hug.
OUR FINISHVoices, three songs, editKorean lines with subtitles, original music for each mood, a cut rebuilt in numbered versions.

Viewer takeaway: A viral format is a camera, a framing and a timing. Measure those, keep them, and put your own people and story inside.

05 / THE FINISH

Picture leads. Sound follows.

Each pair plays the same picture twice. Press one button, then the other; the second picks up where the first left off.

The door

Raw clip: Seedance added its own background music. Final: that music is muted so only one music bed plays, the cute music-box cue.

The face-off

Raw clip: wind and breathing. Final: footsteps, close breathing, a roar over the cut to black, sirens, the sad swell, and Kayden's shout with the music ducked under it.

The last hug

Raw clip: the wave's roar. Final: Jason's line, "Close your eyes. I'm right here," then a fade before the crash.

The mood shift

Earlier cut: the cute song simply hands over. Final: the song tape-stops on Jason's question, a radio boom hits on "Breaking news", the drums come in.

The peak

Earlier cut: the montage song faded out by itself right before the reveal. Final: the drums stay loud, a riser ends exactly on a boom at the reveal.

  1. One background bedNever two music beds at once. Generated clips can carry their own music, so check every clip's sound.
  2. Three songs, three moodsCute, then montage, then apocalypse. Measure each song's loud and quiet parts and land them on story beats.
  3. Designed voicesThe boys' Korean lines come from voice design with fixed settings, so each boy always sounds the same. English subtitles carry the meaning.
  4. Check the renderA sped-up shot once put the picture 1 second behind the sound; a clip hid 2 stray frames. Check every cut after each render.
06 / YOUR TURN

Pick one format. Measure it. Make it yours.

Choose one viral reel. Write down its camera, framing and timing as numbers, plus one story change only you could make. Build a sheet for each character, one start frame, then one clip. Bring all three to a weekend session.
