Every desk hands you tools. None of them hands you the order to use them in — and none of them knows your taste, your facts, or your client. That gap is inside one desk, and it is the same gap in all of them.

Seedance is not Higgsfield’s. The same engines are sold through every desk — which is exactly why no desk can win on models. Who carries what shifts month to month; the shape does not. The zoom is a real camera move on one board: the same bay you were reading shrinks, and the others come into view built exactly the same way. Nothing is redrawn — you are just pulling back.
