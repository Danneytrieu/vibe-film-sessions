02 / THE BRIEF

What we borrowed. What we changed.

A good reference has several jobs. We checked its frames at two per second, then chose which traits to carry forward and which story to invent.

REFERENCECamera and rhythmHistorical-modern humor, contextual framing, grain and busy surroundings.
OUR IDEAEgyptian morningDanney, oversized everyday objects, and a fictional headphone product.
OUR DIRECTIONPerformance and scaleNatural acting, legible physical jokes, a ride that completes its action.
OUR FINISHEdit and soundMusic changes at the headphone fit; grain, SFX and Reelix ending support the picture.

Viewer takeaway: You need a clear reference-role map, approved visual anchors, a shot plan, motion instructions and an edit test. One long prompt cannot reliably settle all five.
