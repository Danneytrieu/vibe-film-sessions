03 / TRY IT

Give every reference a job.

The face controls identity; the costume controls clothing; the location master controls geography; the prop sheet controls physical design and scale. Pick a card, then open the related prompt below.

DANNEY SAYS

“The headphones look too small.”

→
THE VIDEO MODEL NEEDS

Keep the approved scene and design. Make the near earcup larger than the whole gripping hand, while the hand and body stay natural size.

The test: Can you point to the image that already has the right answer, and name exactly what should change? Keep everything else locked.
